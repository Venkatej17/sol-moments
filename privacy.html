Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers Sol Moments Uploader, the tool the Sol Moments team uses to manage its own YouTube channel. Sol Moments Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Sol Moments YouTube channel signs in to Sol Moments Uploader. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube channel management (scope youtube): to upload Shorts, set their title, description, tags and publish time, update the channel banner and description, and delete our own test videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (scope yt-analytics.readonly): to read view, watch-time, like, share and subscriber numbers for the Sol Moments channel's own videos.

It does not access any other Google data, other people's channels, or viewers' personal information.

How data is used and stored

  • Analytics numbers are saved in files on the channel owner's own computer, to decide which Shorts to make next.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on that computer and is never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Analytics files are kept as long as the channel runs and are deleted on request or when the channel closes. YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies. Sol Moments Uploader does not place cookies on any device.

Removing access

You can revoke Sol Moments Uploader's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings → Third-party connections. Revoking it stops all access immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message through the Sol Moments YouTube channel.
